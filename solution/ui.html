mBabel 双语字幕 · 说明Babel Bilingual Captions · About

界面Layout

上半部分是原文 Original:实时语音转写,说话人切换时显示彩色筹码(说话人 1、2…)。

Top pane is the Original: live transcript, with a colored chip marking each speaker change (Speaker 1, 2…).

下半部分是译文 Translation(AI 精翻):完整句子的正式翻译,句号、说话人切换或 3 秒停顿后落定。

Bottom pane is the Translation (AI refined): a proper translation of each full sentence, committed when a sentence ends, the speaker changes, or after 3s of silence.

绿色虚线框 ≈ 快速翻译 Quick draft:句子还在说的时候的草稿译文,持续更新,最终被精翻覆盖。先睹为快,不是最终稿。

The dashed green box ≈ Quick draft is a rough translation that follows speech mid-sentence and keeps updating, then gets replaced by the refined one. Fast, not final.

模型Models

识别:豆包流式语音识别 2.0(二遍识别、热词增强、说话人聚类)。翻译:火山引擎机器翻译大模型,术语表逐句注入。

ASR: Volcengine Seed-ASR 2.0 (two-pass, hotwords, speaker clustering). Translation: Volcengine MT LLM with per-sentence glossary.

隐私Privacy

音频流送往火山引擎做识别和翻译,本地不保存音频;字幕文本只存在这个页面和你导出的文件里。

Audio is processed by Volcengine cloud APIs and never stored locally; captions live only in this page and your exported files.
