mBabel 双语字幕 · 说明 About

界面 Layout

整页是一条对谈稿:每句一块,页边是 Speaker 和时间;上面小字是原文(注释),下面大字是译文(正文)。底部直播条:原声 = 实时识别,快译 = 草稿翻译,句子落定后被正式译文覆盖。

A single bilingual script: each turn shows the speaker and time in the margin, the original in small type (annotation) above the translation in large type (the text). The live strip at the bottom carries live recognition and the rough draft, replaced by the refined translation at sentence end.

语言视图 Language view

顶栏"显示"选的是你阅读的语言:选中文或 English 后隐藏原文行,和你语言相同的句子直接显示原文,不同的显示译文。

The view selector picks YOUR reading language: originals hide, sentences in your language show as-is, the rest show their translation.

模型 Models

识别:豆包流式语音识别 2.0(二遍识别、热词增强、说话人聚类)。翻译:火山引擎机器翻译大模型,术语表逐句注入。

ASR: Volcengine Seed-ASR 2.0 (two-pass, hotwords, speaker clustering). Translation: Volcengine MT LLM with per-sentence glossary.

隐私 Privacy

音频流送往火山引擎做识别和翻译,本地不保存音频;字幕文本只存在这个页面和你导出的文件里。

Audio is processed by Volcengine cloud APIs and never stored locally; captions live only in this page and your exported files.

识别纠错 ASR correction

修的是语音识别听错的词(原文里的错字),不是翻译。写法:错词=正确词,例:CKG=CKB。即时对后续句子生效,不回改历史;退出时存入术语表。 This fixes what the ASR misheard (wrong words in the ORIGINAL line), not translation. Format: wrong=right, e.g. CKG=CKB. Applies to future sentences only; persists on exit for the next meeting.
