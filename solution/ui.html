mBabel 双语字幕 · 说明 About

界面 Layout

整页是一条对谈稿:每句一块,页边是 Speaker 和时间;上面小字是原文(注释),下面大字是译文(正文)。底部直播条:原声 = 实时识别,快译 = 草稿翻译,句子落定后被正式译文覆盖。

A single bilingual script: each turn shows the speaker and time in the margin, the original in small type (annotation) above the translation in large type (the text). The live strip at the bottom carries live recognition and the rough draft, replaced by the refined translation at sentence end.

语言视图 Language view

顶栏"显示"选的是你阅读的语言:选中文或 English 后隐藏原文行,和你语言相同的句子直接显示原文,不同的显示译文。

The view selector picks YOUR reading language: originals hide, sentences in your language show as-is, the rest show their translation.

语言对 Language pairs

支持中↔EN、EN↔VI、中↔VI。中英模式有逐字快译、热词和说话人分离；含越南语的模式按整句出字，无热词和说话人分离。识别纠错和翻译术语表在三种模式中都照常使用。

Supports ZH↔EN, EN↔VI, and ZH↔VI. ZH↔EN includes live drafts, hotwords, and speaker separation. Vietnamese pairs caption whole sentences without hotwords or speaker separation. Recognition corrections and the translation glossary remain active in all three modes.

模型 Models

识别:豆包流式语音识别 2.0(二遍识别、热词增强、说话人聚类)。翻译:火山引擎机器翻译大模型,术语表逐句注入。

ASR: Volcengine Seed-ASR 2.0 (two-pass, hotwords, speaker clustering). Translation: Volcengine MT LLM with per-sentence glossary.

隐私 Privacy

音频流送往火山引擎做识别和翻译,本地不保存音频;字幕文本只存在这个页面和你导出的文件里。

Audio is processed by Volcengine cloud APIs and never stored locally; captions live only in this page and your exported files.

识别纠错 ASR correction

修的是语音识别听错的词(原文里的错字),不是翻译。写法:错词=正确词,例:CKG=CKB。即时对后续句子生效并立即存入术语表,不回改历史。 This fixes what the ASR misheard (wrong words in the ORIGINAL line), not translation. Format: wrong=right, e.g. CKG=CKB. It applies to future sentences and is saved immediately; history is unchanged.

分享·导出 Share / Export

分享 Share

打开链接的人能看到实时双语字幕，也可以在自己浏览器里导出。链接在程序关闭后失效。

Anyone with the link can watch live bilingual captions and export from their own browser. Links expire when the app stops.

The public link exposes the full transcript. Share only with attendees.

格式 Format

Speaker 命名 Speaker names

Click a name to jump to and highlight their lines behind this dialog.

本次会议还没有识别到说话人。No speakers detected yet.

输过的名字会记在本机浏览器,下次点输入框直接下拉选择;不会按编号自动填,因为编号和人不绑定。命名后页面上的 Speaker 标签也会换成名字。

Typed names are remembered locally for quick selection and update the speaker labels on the page. Names are never auto-assigned to a cluster number.

删除只会移除下次可选的名字,不会改写本场字幕。

Deleting a saved name only removes the suggestion; it does not change this meeting.
